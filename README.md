# DMflux

> Click analytics for WhatsApp marketers — prove your reach, charge more for ads.

[https://dmflux.app](https://dmflux.app)

DMflux turns WhatsApp campaign links into verifiable click evidence: unique
visitors, devices, countries, and click timestamps, packaged into
client-ready public reports. Built for WhatsApp TV channels, influencers,
and businesses in the African WhatsApp-first market.

## What it does

- **Campaign link tracking** — short links (dmflux.app/c/{slug}) that redirect
  to any WhatsApp destination while recording every click.
- **Unique visitor counting** — first-party device identity, deduplicated
  across repeat taps.
- **Health Score** — a 0–100 audience-quality rating per campaign, weighing
  click authenticity and traffic consistency.
- **Fraud detection** — bots, crawlers, and prefetch requests filtered at the
  redirect edge.
- **Public reports** — shareable read-only receipts for advertisers, no login
  needed.
- **PDF & CSV export**, **CPC/ROI calculator**, **QR codes**.
